Nolima Accounting 1.4.0

- Delete: invoices, supplier bills, quotations (including ones already invoiced), receipts, payments,
  expenses, money received, transfers, journals, accounts, departments and users. Deleting an invoice also
  removes its accounting entries and puts the stock back. Invoices with payments: delete the payment first.
  Only Administrators and Accountants can delete; every deletion is recorded in the audit trail.
- VAT summary now prints as a Tax Liability Report: tax agency, tax jurisdiction, rate, net amount and tax
  (Ministry of Tourism levy, MRA VAT on sales and purchases, no-tax purchases) plus the VAT payable to MRA.
- Tourism levy summary now prints as the Ministry of Tourism - Tax Summary Report (lines 9, 10, 1, 11, 17,
  19, 20, 21, 18, 22).
- New report: Sales by Product/Service Summary. Sales by Customer Summary in the same style.
- All reports print with the company name, report title, period (e.g. "August 2026") and Accrual Basis date.
